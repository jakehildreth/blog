Jake Hildreth is a dedicated husband, fun-loving father, and seasoned IT professional with nearly 25 years of experience. As Principal Security Consultant at Semperis, Jake helps organizations fortify their digital defenses against Active Directory incursions. His open-source tools (Locksmith, BlueTuxedo, and PowerPUG!) are designed to lighten the load for overworked AD administrators by making security more accessible and manageable. Jake's expertise is further underscored by his CISSP certification and Microsoft MVP status, both testaments to his wide base of knowledge and commitment to cybersecurity excellence.
